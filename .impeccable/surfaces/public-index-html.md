# Squadup app (public/)

Scope: the whole mobile web app (landing, join, Home, Plan, Polls, Rides, Money, Crew, sheets). Mode: Operate (the landing page leans Persuade inside the same world).

Audience and job: teen friend groups on phones, often at night or in a car; know what's happening, what needs you, and act (RSVP, accept a pickup, vote, pay). Judges watch a demo video.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every hangout is a departure. Home is your boarding pass, Rides is a departure board, and status flips like split-flap letters. It refuses the stock dashboard: rounded soft cards, gradient hero, icon-box grids.

OWN-WORLD: one violet-black tonal ramp for every neutral; logo violet #7030E0 fills primary actions and the pass header strip; lavender #A19DF0 marks codes, active tabs and what's yours (ringed). Split-flap tiles, dashed tear lines with punched notches, one 1px rule weight, struck = done or declined. Big Shoulders Display for pass and board headlines, Archivo for UI, JetBrains Mono only for times, codes, seats and money.

STORY: open the link and in one glance know when, where, your car and seat, and what needs you; then act in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: app bar (logo mark, squad name, crew, menu); boarding pass with the date as a 44px headline, venue, DEPART / CAR / SEAT fields, tear line, RSVP stub; Needs you list below; bottom tabs Home, Plan, Polls, Rides, Money.

FORM: Departures, position 3 of my ordered list, seed:f442b05ed1a15705. Signature interaction: split-flap status cells on the rides board flip letter by letter when a ride's status changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
